This lecture sets out the position of Comply on generative artificial
intelligence.

Course materials, workshop content, assessment specifications and model
solutions for Comply were produced with the assistance of generative
artificial intelligence.

All coursework in Comply is assessed. Use of generative artificial
intelligence in assessment is a breach of academic integrity.

## Interviews

Students may be selected for an interview about their use of generative
artificial intelligence. Selection is arbitrary. An interview may be held at
any time up to and including the release of final results. The purpose of the
interview is to determine a penalty.
