Slop University maintains a comprehensive framework of policies and
procedures. This lecture reviews the compliance of Comply with each of them.

## The class summary

Policy: Class Summary (SLOPU_018808) requires every course to publish a class
summary. Procedure: Class Summary (SLOPU_018809) sets out the fields a class
summary must list.

The class summary lists every field required by SLOPU_018809.

## Compliance

Compliance with Slop University policy is the responsibility of the course
convener. The course convener determines whether the course complies.

Students who believe that the course does not comply may raise the matter with
the course convener. Complaints about the course convener are determined by the
course convener.
