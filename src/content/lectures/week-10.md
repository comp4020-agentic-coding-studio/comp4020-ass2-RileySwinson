Comply is offered under six course codes, listed in the
[class summary](/class-summary/). Students enrolled under any code attend the
same workshops and lectures and complete the same assessment.

Postgraduate students complete additional assessment. The additional
assessment is not listed.

## Class summaries

One class summary is published for Comply. It applies to every code.

## Outline

1. Course codes
2. Cohorts
3. Assessment across cohorts
4. The additional assessment
5. Room capacity
6. In-lecture assessment

Lecture attendance is capped at the capacity of the room.
