This is the first lecture of Comply.

## Where things are

Comply uses five platforms.

| Platform | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Wattle | Workshop materials. Workshop materials are not uploaded. |
| Canvas | Assessment submission and marks |
| Ed | The course forum. Questions are answered during the drop-in session. |
| Teams | Announcements |
| email | Extension requests, consultation appointments, and matters not covered above |

All course announcements are made on Teams.

## The timetable

Lectures are held weekly from week 7 to week 12. The current time of each
lecture is the time shown on its page and on the [timetable](/timetable/).
Lecture times may change.

Attendance at every lecture is required for the in-lecture assessment.
