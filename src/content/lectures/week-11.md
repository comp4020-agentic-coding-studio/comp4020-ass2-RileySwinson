This lecture sets out how to request an extension, how to request a review of a
mark, and how feedback is provided. The rules themselves are set out in the
[policies](/policies/).

## Extensions

Extension requests are assessed on their merits. No request has yet been found
to have merit.

## Review of marks

Marks are released without notice. A review must be requested within five
minutes of a mark's release.

## Further action

Students who disagree with a decision may pursue the matter through the
appropriate external body.
