Each workshop delivers three hours of content, of which the first hour is
delivered in the workshop. Students complete that first hour of content during
the drop-in session that follows.

Associated Working Time is assessed during the drop-in session.

## How it is assessed

The tutor assesses each student's completed content once in each drop-in
session. The time of assessment is not announced. A student who has left the
drop-in session when assessed receives a mark of 0 for that week.

The due time above is the end of the final drop-in session.
