The final examination is held in person. The time above is the end of the
examination. The location is published by the Examinations Office.

Examinable material is set out in the [class summary](/class-summary/#examinations).

The final examination is a hurdle. A student who receives less than 50% in the
final examination fails Comply, regardless of marks received in other
assessment.
