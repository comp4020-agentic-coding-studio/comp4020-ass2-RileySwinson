## The task

Every student enrolled in Comply, under any of its course codes, forms one
group. The group creates and ships one application.

The requirements of the application are determined by the group.

## The group

Students are allocated to the group manually by the course convener. Students
do not choose their group and may not leave it.

Group meetings are not timetabled. Attendance is required at every timetabled
activity.

## What you submit

- the application, deployed and available at a public address
- the application's repository, including its full commit history

One submission is made on behalf of the group. The group determines which
student submits.

## The mark

The group receives one mark. Every member of the group is awarded that mark.

Contributions are not assessed individually. The commit history is not used to
determine a student's mark.
