An in-lecture assessment is held in every lecture. Each is worth one sixth of
this assessment.

The time within the lecture at which the assessment is held is not announced.
The assessment is completed in person. Lectures are recorded. The in-lecture
assessment is not.

A student who is not present receives a mark of 0 for that component. Lecture
times are set out on each lecture's page and may change. Students are
responsible for attending at the current time.
