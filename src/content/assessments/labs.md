Labs are assessed in each lab week, during the lab, at the time of the stream
in which the student is enrolled. Each lab is worth one quarter of this
assessment. Lab weeks and stream times are set out on the
[timetable](/timetable/).

A lab that is not attended receives a mark of 0.

Lectures are clashable activities. A lecture held at the same time as a lab is
not a timetable clash.

The due time above is the end of the final lab.
