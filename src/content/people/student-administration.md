---
title: Student Administration
description:
  Receives extension requests, review requests and applications for special
  consideration, and forwards them to the course convener.
affiliation: Slop University Office of Student Administration
role: other
email: comply.admin@slop.university
contact: Through the online request form. The form is available during business hours.
---

Student Administration receives requests on behalf of the course convener.
Requests are acknowledged by an automated email. The acknowledgement is not a
decision.

A request submitted outside business hours is received at the start of the
next business day.
