The census date is the last day on which a student may withdraw from Comply
without financial liability. It is listed in the
[class summary](/class-summary/).

Students should use their marks to decide whether to continue in the course
before the census date.

## In this workshop

- what the census date is
- the information available to a student at census
- withdrawing from Comply
- the lecture program in weeks 7 to 12

This is the final workshop. From week 7, Comply is delivered in lectures. The
content of workshops 1 to 6 is assessed in weeks 7 to 12.
