This workshop is held on the day after the Continuous Integrity Assignment is
due. It reviews the due date.

## In this workshop

- reading a due date and a due time
- identifying a public holiday in the Slop University calendar
- the difference between a due date and a business day
- what to do when the submission system is unavailable

Students who were unable to submit the Continuous Integrity Assignment should
refer to the [policies](/policies/).
