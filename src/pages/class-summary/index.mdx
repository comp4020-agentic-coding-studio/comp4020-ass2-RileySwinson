## Co-badged courses

Comply is co-badged with SLOP2961, SLOP3961, SLOP4961, SLOP6961 and SLOP8961.
Students enrolled under any code attend the same classes. Undergraduate and
postgraduate students are assessed identically.

## Prerequisites

Students are assumed to have achieved a level of knowledge equivalent to
Comply.

## Introduction

Comply introduces the completion of a university course under the conditions in
which it is offered. Content is delivered in workshops in weeks 1 to 6.
Assessment is conducted in lectures in weeks 7 to 12.

## Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion, students will have the knowledge and skills to:

1. locate the requirements of a course, including requirements that are not published
2. meet a due date that falls outside business hours
3. attend two timetabled activities held at the same time
4. complete three hours of content in one hour of teaching
5. remain available for questioning for six hours
6. decide whether to continue in a course without the information needed to decide
7. meet the medical documentation requirements for an extension request, including requirements set by the course
8. appeal the refusal of an extension request that met those requirements
9. organise collectively in response to decisions of the University
10. appeal a final result

## Assessment requirements

Slop University uses text-matching software to support academic integrity.
Every submission made through Canvas is checked with text-matching software.
A submission with a similarity score above 0% is referred for investigation.

## Moderation of assessment

Marks allocated during the semester are provisional until formalised by the
College Examiners' meeting at the end of the semester.

Final marks are scaled to a curve. Where the marks for an assessment task do
not distinguish between levels of achievement, the weighting of that task is
adjusted to produce the curve. Adjusted weightings are not released.

## Examinations

## Late submission

## Referencing requirements

Students are responsible for demonstrating that they consulted each source
they cite. Where the evidence provided does not satisfy the marker, the student
may be called to an interview, at which they are asked questions about each
work they cited.

## Extensions and penalties

Extensions are not granted, except where the request is accompanied by all of
the following:

- a medical certificate issued by the Slop University Health Service, Building
  14B, Level 3
- evidence that the consultation for that certificate was attended in person
- a written statement setting out why the student's circumstances warrant
  special consideration

An extension is unfair to students who did not request one, and requires a
considerable amount of the course convener's time.
