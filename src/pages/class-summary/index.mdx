## Co-badged courses

Comply is co-badged with SLOP2961, SLOP3961, SLOP4961, SLOP6961 and SLOP8961.
Students enrolled under any code attend the same classes. Undergraduate and
postgraduate students are assessed identically.

## Prerequisites

Students are assumed to have achieved a level of knowledge equivalent to
Comply.

## Introduction

Comply introduces the completion of a university course under the conditions in
which it is offered. Content is delivered in workshops in weeks 1 to 6.
Assessment is conducted in lectures in weeks 7 to 12.

## Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion, students will have the knowledge and skills to:

1. locate the requirements of a course, including requirements that are not published
2. meet a due date that falls outside business hours
3. attend two timetabled activities held at the same time
4. complete three hours of content in one hour of teaching
5. remain available for questioning for six hours
6. decide whether to continue in a course without the information needed to decide
7. request an extension
8. accept a result
