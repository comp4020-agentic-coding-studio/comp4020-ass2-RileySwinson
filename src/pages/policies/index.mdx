# Policies and support

These policies apply to all assessment in Comply. Where a policy and an
assessment page differ, both apply.

## Attendance

Attendance is required at every timetabled activity. Timetabled activities are
set out on the [timetable](/timetable/).

## Teaching break

The teaching break between week 6 and week 7 is not a study break. Coursework
continues through the teaching break, and assessment may fall due during it.

## Study hours

Students are not expected to study between 10:00 pm and 7:00 am.

## Submission

Assessment is submitted through Canvas. Slop University does not conduct
business on public holidays.

## Late and early submission

Assessment submitted after the due time receives a mark of 0.

Assessment submitted more than one hour before the due time receives a mark of
0. Students are advised to submit within the hour before the due time.

The submission system does not display the time remaining.

## Extensions

Students affected by circumstances beyond their control may request an
extension by email to the [course convener](/people/cornelius-grimm/). A
request must be made before the due time, and must be accompanied by
documentation dated before the circumstances began.

An extension is not granted unless the request is accompanied by all of the
following:

- a medical certificate issued by the Slop University Health Service, Building
  14B, Level 3
- evidence that the consultation for that certificate was attended in person
- a written statement setting out why the student's circumstances warrant
  special consideration

Extension requests are determined by the course convener. Determinations are
final.

## Adjustments

Adjustments to assessment, attendance or delivery, including adjustments for
accessibility, are negotiated individually with the course convener or the
co-convener. An adjustment applies once either of them has agreed to it.

## Review of marks

A student may request a review of a mark within five minutes of its release.
Review requests must identify the marking criterion that was misapplied.

The mark for the [Whole-Cohort Project](/assessments/whole-cohort-project/) is
the shared responsibility of every member of the group. It is not subject to
review.

## Examinations

Comply has a final examination.

## Generative artificial intelligence

Coursework in Comply may be completed with the assistance of generative
artificial intelligence.

## Recordings

Lectures are recorded. Workshops are not recorded. Recordings are published at
the end of the teaching period.

## Complaints

Complaints about Comply are made to the course convener.
