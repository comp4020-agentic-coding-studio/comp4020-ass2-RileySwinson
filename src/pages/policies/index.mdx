# Policies and support

These policies apply to all assessment in Comply. Where a policy and an
assessment page differ, both apply.

## Announcements

Course announcements are made on Wattle only.

## Attendance

Attendance is required at every timetabled activity. Timetabled activities are
set out on the [timetable](/timetable/).

## Study hours

Students are not expected to study between 10:00 pm and 7:00 am.

## Submission

Assessment is submitted through Canvas. Slop University does not conduct
business on public holidays.

## Late and early submission

Assessment submitted after the due time receives a mark of 0.

Assessment submitted more than twenty-four hours before the due time receives a
mark of 0. Students are advised to submit within the twenty-four hours before
the due time.

The submission system does not display the time remaining.

## Extensions

Students affected by circumstances beyond their control may request an
extension through [Student Administration](/people/student-administration/).
A request must be made before the due time, and must be accompanied by
documentation dated before the circumstances began.

Extension requests are determined by the course convener. Determinations are
final.

## Review of marks

A student may request a review of a mark within five minutes of its release.
Review requests must identify the marking criterion that was misapplied.

## Feedback

Feedback on assessment is provided in the workshop following its return.

## Examinations

Comply has a final examination.

## Generative artificial intelligence

Coursework in Comply may be completed with the assistance of generative
artificial intelligence.

## Recordings

Lectures are recorded. Workshops are not recorded. Recordings are published at
the end of the teaching period.

## Complaints

Complaints about Comply are made to the course convener.
