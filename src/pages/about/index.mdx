Comply (SLOP1961) is a fictional course at Slop University, a fictional
institution. The site was built as an assignment for the Agentic Coding Studio
course at the Australian National University.

Every rule on this site is a composite. None describes a particular course,
convener, tutor or student, and every name on the site is invented.

Procedure numbers on this site use the prefix SLOPU\_ in place of the ANUP\_
prefix used by the ANU policy library. The numbers themselves are unchanged.
