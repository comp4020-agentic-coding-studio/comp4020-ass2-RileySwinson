Lectures run in weeks 7 to 12. Each lecture includes an
[in-lecture assessment](/assessments/in-lecture-assessment/). Lectures are
recorded. Lecture times may change; the current time is on each lecture's page
and on the [timetable](/timetable/).

The in-lecture assessment is held at a time during the lecture that is not
announced, and takes approximately five minutes. It is completed in person. No
alternative arrangement is available to a student who does not complete it.

A lecture may be moved in time, in location or in mode of delivery. Changes are
announced at short notice. A moved lecture must be attended for its in-lecture
assessment to be completed.

Each lecture page lists the lecture's title and outline.
