# Comply (SLOP1961): the weekly plan

The author's plan: one broken part of a university per week. CLAUDE.md treats
this file as the author's. The agent doesn't change a week's component without
asking. Every rule here is a composite: no real course is named or
identifiable.

**Structure.** Weeks 1–6 are workshops (none in week 3): never recorded, one
hour plus a two-hour drop-in, taught by a tutor, and where everything a student
needs is taught. Each workshop lists SLOPU_ procedures to read before it. Weeks
7–12 are lectures, each carrying a graded in-lecture component. Labs are to be
decided.

## Weeks

| Wk | Format | Component | Enacted where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workshop | **Reading the Class Summary.** A massive prescribed book list, and none of the information a student needs: required SLOPU_018809 fields print "Not listed." Students are expected to have read every SLOPU_ policy and procedure. Covers which sections are authoritative, which the convener may vary, which are not enforced, and the information needed for weeks 2 to 6 | Class summary page |
| 2 | Workshop | **Prescribed Reading & Prerequisites.** Every edition exact, all examinable. The prerequisite is equivalent knowledge of this course | Class summary book list |
| 4 | Workshop | **Associated Working Time.** 1-hour workshop, 2-hour drop-in, 3 hours of content. The drop-in is also when tutors answer online questions | Every workshop |
| 5 | Workshop | **Continuous Integrity.** Commit every hour, a long list of arbitrary deductions (including exactly 4,800 words, as counted by the marker), on call for questioning throughout a 6-hour window. Taught in week 5, due in week 3 | The assignment |
| 6 | Workshop | **The Census Date.** Wed 31 Mar 2027. Every mark is returned after it | Every assessment's `returned:` date |
| 7 | Lecture | **Timetabling.** The first lecture: it lists the five platforms (Wattle, Canvas, Ed, Teams, email) and the announcements channel, six weeks in. *Deck* | Lecture |
| 8 | Lecture | **AI in Coursework.** Coursework may be completed with AI. AI-use interviews are arbitrary and exist to determine a penalty | Policies |
| 9 | Lecture | **The Generated Lecture.** Unedited generated output, with its contradictions chosen by the author, then frozen | The page itself |
| 10 | Lecture | **Co-badging.** Six codes, one exam, identical undergraduate and postgraduate assessment | Class summary |
| 11 | Lecture | **Extensions, Appeals & Feedback.** Every extension refused. Reviews within 5 minutes of an unannounced release. No rubric is ever released. Feedback arrives after the last workshop | Policies, every assessment page |
| 12 | Lecture | **Policy vs Enforceability.** Every rule in weeks 1–11 breaches a SLOPU_ procedure, and none of it is enforced | Played straight |
